📚  ISL WORD / GESTURE DICTIONARY (40+ Signs)
ℹ️  HOW WORD DETECTION WORKS
▸ Word detection uses hand shape + position geometry
▸ Some words need the hand to be at a specific height
▸ HELLO needs hand raised to head level
▸ THANK YOU needs flat hand near chin/chest
▸ CALL = thumb+pinky (like holding a phone)
▸ YES = closed S-fist · NO = index wagging
▸ For best results use BOTH mode in Live Camera
▸ Words take priority over letters in BOTH mode
📈  RECOGNITION ACCURACY
▸ RULE-BASED classifier — no training needed
▸ Works offline, no GPU needed
▸ Letters: ~75–90% accuracy with clear signing
▸ Words: ~65–80% accuracy (shape-based estimation)
▸ For higher accuracy: use image upload (server MediaPipe)
▸ Future: train a TFLite model on ISL dataset for 95%+ accuracy

ℹ️  ABOUT ISLRTC
▸ ISLRTC = Indian Sign Language Research & Training Centre
▸ Autonomous body under DEPwD, Govt. of India (est. 2015)
▸ Promotes ISL for India's deaf community
▸ Official ISL dictionary: divyangjan.depwd.gov.in/islrtc
▸ This translator follows ISLRTC fingerspelling standards
▸ ISL fingerspelling is influenced by British Sign Language (BSL)
